Files used in this game (renamed):
  thump.ogg      <- Audio/drop_004.ogg
  boop.ogg       <- Audio/back_002.ogg
  chime.ogg      <- Audio/confirmation_001.ogg
  seal.ogg       <- Audio/bong_001.ogg
  whoosh.ogg     <- Audio/minimize_003.ogg
  complete.ogg   <- Audio/confirmation_004.ogg
  sparkle.ogg    <- Audio/glass_001.ogg
  pickup.ogg     <- Audio/click_002.ogg
  stamp.ogg      <- Audio/drop_002.ogg
  ting.ogg       <- Audio/glass_005.ogg

The last two were chosen by measurement rather than by name, so each is
audibly its own sound and not a near-copy of one already in use:

  stamp.ogg   0.20 s, 0.3 ms attack, spectral centroid 970 Hz — the rubber
              stamp meeting the envelope. Kept clear of thump.ogg (the mark
              press: drop_004, 841 Hz but 0.29 s) so the two stampings of a
              letter — the mark on the paper and the frank on the envelope —
              do not sound like one another.
  ting.ogg    0.13 s, centroid 2846 Hz, 76% of its energy above 2 kHz — the
              progress mark filling. glass_002 was the other candidate and was
              rejected for sitting on top of sparkle.ogg (glass_001, 2521 Hz):
              at 2531 Hz it was the same sound twice. glass_005 is brighter and
              less than half as long, so it reads as its own event.
